Garment Buying House in Dhaka — Al Esharah Fashion Ltd

Al Esharah Fashion Ltd operates a professional garment buying house in Dhaka to help international buyers source high-quality apparel from Bangladesh. We act as your local apparel sourcing agent, managing supplier selection, sampling, costing, quality control and export logistics so you can focus on sales and design.

What our Buying House does

  • Supplier sourcing & qualification — we identify and audit factories that match your product, budget and compliance requirements.
  • Sampling & development — technical packs, prototyping, fit sessions and pre-production samples.
  • Costing & negotiation — material sourcing, trim quotation, and competitive factory pricing.
  • Quality control & inspection — in-line and final inspections, AQL checks and corrective actions.
  • Logistics & export — packaging, documentation, freight forwarding coordination and on-time shipments.

Why choose a Buying House in Bangladesh?

Bangladesh is one of the world’s largest apparel exporters. A local buying house like Al Esharah Fashion helps buyers reduce risk, shorten lead times, and ensure compliance. Our local presence in Dhaka allows us to:

  1. Access a network of reliable factories for woven, knit and specialty garments.
  2. Perform hands-on quality checks and real-time issue resolution.
  3. Negotiate better costs by consolidating material and production volumes.

Typical services & pricing model

We offer flexible engagement models: project-based sourcing, ongoing buyer representation, or per-order buying house services. Pricing depends on order size, complexity and service level — please contact us for a tailored quote.

Sample timeline

Sampling: 7–21 days • Production: 30–60 days (depending on order) • Shipping: 7–21 days

Case study (example)

We worked with a European private-label brand to source 5,000 Panjabi units. Services provided: factory selection, pre-production sample, inline QC and final container inspection. Result: 98% first-pass quality and on-time shipment.
