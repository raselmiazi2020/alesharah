    Why Build Your Career in Bangladesh's Garments Sector?

    Bangladesh is the world's second-largest readymade garments (RMG) exporter, and Al Esharah Fashion Ltd takes pride in being part of this thriving industry. With over 4,000 export-oriented factories, the apparel sector contributes more than 80% of the country's total export earnings. Jobs in garments manufacturing offer excellent growth opportunities, competitive salaries, and exposure to global buyers. Whether you specialize in woven garments, knit items, or denim products, a career in RMG promises stability and professional development.

    Current Job Market in Gazipur & Dhaka EPZ

    Our factory is strategically located in Gazipur, the industrial hub near Dhaka. This region hosts hundreds of garments factories and is known for high production capacity. Al Esharah Fashion Ltd regularly recruits for positions like Merchandiser, Quality Controller, Industrial Engineer, Production Manager, Compliance Officer, HR & Admin Executive, Sample Coordinator, Fabric Technologist, Cutting Supervisor, Finishing In-charge, and Store Keeper. If you are looking for garments job circular 2026, you have come to the right place.

    Skills in Demand for RMG Careers

    Employers seek candidates with expertise in Lean Manufacturing, Six Sigma, SMV calculation, line balancing, production planning, fabric inspection (4-point system), AQL standard, social compliance audits (BSCI, WRAP, SEDEX, Accord/Alliance), ERP software, and strong English communication. Knowledge of woven and knit fabric construction, garments washing techniques, and international shipping documentation is a plus. We value hardworking, innovative individuals ready to contribute to our vision of excellence.

    Al Esharah Fashion Ltd - Your Trusted Partner

    Established as a leading garments manufacturer in Bangladesh, Al Esharah specializes in high-quality woven shirts, trousers, jackets, and knitwear for top European and American brands. Our state-of-the-art facilities include automated cutting machines, modern sewing lines with lockstitch and overlock machines, and advanced finishing units. We prioritize worker safety, fair wages, and sustainable manufacturing. By joining our team, you become part of a company that values professional integrity, teamwork, and continuous improvement.

    How to Get a Job in Garments Sector in Bangladesh

    To secure a position in the RMG industry, focus on building a strong CV highlighting relevant experience, technical skills, and any certifications like PGD in Garment Manufacturing Technology or Diploma in Textile Engineering. Stay updated with bdjobs.com and company career pages. Networking through industry events and LinkedIn can also help. At Al Esharah, we believe in equal opportunity and encourage female candidates to apply for supervisory and management roles.

    Employee Benefits & Work Environment

    We offer attractive remuneration packages, festival bonuses, provident fund, medical allowance, transportation facilities, and a safe workplace with clean drinking water, fire safety measures, and regular health camps. Our HR team ensures compliance with Bangladesh Labor Law 2006 and amendments. We also provide training programs for skill development, leadership workshops, and career progression plans.

    Future of RMG Industry in Bangladesh

    With the government's Vision 2041 and the increasing demand for ethical fashion, Bangladesh's garments industry is poised for sustainable growth. Automation, digital printing, AI-based quality control, and green factories are transforming the sector. Therefore, professionals with knowledge in Industry 4.0, sustainable textiles, and circular fashion will have a competitive edge. Al Esharah is committed to adopting modern technologies and nurturing talent for tomorrow's challenges.
