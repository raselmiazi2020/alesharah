Our Complete Garment Printing Services

We specialize in all major garment print types. Below is a detailed breakdown of each technique, its applications, and why it's ideal for your products.

Screen Printing (Traditional & Advanced)

Screen printing is one of the most versatile and durable methods for fabric decoration. Our unit handles various screen printing subtypes:

  • HD Screen Print: High-density prints with raised, 3D-like texture — perfect for bold logos and designs on jackets, polo shirts, and sweatshirts.
  • Silicon Screen Print: Soft, rubbery feel with excellent elasticity; ideal for sportswear and leggings.
  • 3D Screen Print: Puff/foam effect that stands out from the fabric surface — great for adding dimension to kids' wear and fashion tops.
  • Rubber & Semi-Rubber Print: Thick, glossy finish with high opacity; used for heavy fabrics like denim, bags, and workwear.
  • Pigment Print: Water-based and eco-friendly, offering soft hand feel on cotton and blends — ideal for everyday t-shirts and kameez.
  • Photo Print (Process Print): High-resolution photographic reproductions using CMYK halftones; perfect for detailed artwork on fatua and tunics.
  • Discharge Print: Removes dye from colored fabric to create soft, breathable prints — excellent for dark garments.

Digital Textile Printing (Direct to Fabric / DTG)

Digital printing allows for infinite color variation, no minimum order quantity, and fast sampling. We offer:

  • Cotton Digital Printing: Reactive inks for vibrant, long-lasting prints on 100% cotton — ideal for ladies tops, kameez, and men's polo shirts.
  • Polyester Sublimation Printing: Transfer printing using heat to infuse dye into polyester fabrics. Perfect for activewear, sweat jackets, and leggings with full-coverage designs.
  • Mixed Fabric Digital: Custom solutions for blends (cotton-poly, viscose, etc.) using optimized inks.

Specialty & Value-Added Printing

  • Flock & Foil Printing: Raised velvet texture and metallic shine — popular for ethnic wear like Pakistani kalbi, thobe, and 3pcs kameez.
  • Glow-in-the-Dark & Reflective Print: Safety wear and night-active sportswear.
  • Puff / Foam Print: 3D raised effect that adds tactile appeal.
  • Burnout Print: Creates semi-transparent designs using chemical paste — stylish on viscose and rayon fabrics.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) About Garment Printing

Q1: What is the best printing method for t-shirts?

A: For large quantities and solid designs, screen printing is most cost-effective. For full-color, small batches, digital DTG is ideal.

Q2: Do you offer eco-friendly printing?

A: Yes, we use water-based pigments, Oeko-Tex certified inks, and offer GOTS-certified organic printing on request.

Q3: Can you print on dark garments?

A: Absolutely. We use discharge printing, high-opacity screen inks, and white underbase for digital printing to ensure vibrant results on black/navy fabrics.

Q4: What is your typical lead time?

A: Sample: 2-3 days. Bulk production: 7-15 days depending on quantity and complexity.

Q5: Do you provide shipping worldwide?

A: Yes, we export printed garments and fabrics to Panjab (India/Pakistan), Kabul (Afghanistan), UAE, UK, USA, and other countries through reliable freight forwarders.
