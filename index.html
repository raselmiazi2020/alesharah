Al Esharah Fashion Ltd — Premium Garments Manufacturer in Bangladesh

Al Esharah Fashion Ltd is a premium garments manufacturer in Bangladesh with factory direct export for Panjabi, Saudi thobe, Omani khaleej, Dubai thobe, bomber jacket, ladies 3 piece kameez, formal shirts and export apparel.

Why Choose Al Esharah Fashion Ltd

Al Esharah Fashion Ltd specializes in Bangladesh garment manufacturing. We focus on quality, compliance, and competitive export pricing. Our factory produces ready-made garments for buyers who need dependable sourcing, rapid sampling, and effective production management.

As a trusted garments manufacturer in Bangladesh, we work with global brands and private label customers. Our capabilities include Panjabi, thobe, jackets, womens wear, casual shirts, formal shirts, and finished export apparel for wholesale and distribution.

  • Factory direct manufacturing with BSCI and ethical compliance.
  • Custom apparel production, private label clothing, and bulk garment orders.
  • Design support, material sourcing, and on-time export shipments.

We maintain a modern Bangladesh factory and experienced merchandising team. Our quality inspections reduce defects and help deliver consistent garments ready for retail shelves, catalogs, and online stores. Customers choose Al Esharah Fashion Ltd for stable pricing, efficient order tracking, and manufacturing expertise in woven, knit, and tailored garments.
