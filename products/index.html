Our Premium Product Categories

Al Esharah Fashion Ltd takes pride in producing high-demand garments that meet international quality standards. Here are our specialty products:

👔 Panjabi (Traditional & Modern)

We manufacture premium quality Panjabi for men and boys in various styles — casual, formal, wedding, and festive wear. Our Panjabis feature fine stitching, premium cotton and linen fabrics, and customizable embroidery patterns. Perfect for local Bangladeshi brands and export markets with significant South Asian diaspora.

🕌 Saudi Thobe & Dubai Thobe (Arabic Garments)

Specialized in producing Saudi Thobe, Dubai Thobe, Kandura, and Dishdasha for Middle Eastern markets. Our thobes are made from lightweight, breathable fabrics suitable for Gulf climates. We offer various collar styles, cuff designs, and custom embroidery options. A trusted supplier for Middle Eastern buyers sourcing from Bangladesh.

🧥 Bomber Jackets & Shackets

Premium quality bomber jackets and shackets (shirt-jackets) for men and women. Our outerwear collection includes nylon bombers, cotton shackets, denim jackets, and custom designs. In-house embroidery and print units allow us to add logos, patches, and custom artwork. Popular among streetwear brands and fashion retailers.

👗 Ladies Tops & Women's Wear

Comprehensive range of ladies tops, blouses, tunics, and casual wear. From basic t-shirts to embroidered blouses, we cater to both fast fashion and high-end women's apparel. Our handwork unit adds value with beadwork, sequin, and lace details.

👕 Formal Shirts & Corporate Wear

Precision-tailored formal shirts for men and women. Perfect for corporate uniforms, retail brands, and institutional buyers. We offer a wide range of collar styles, cuff types, and fabric options including easy-iron and wrinkle-free finishes.

Frequently Asked Questions – Al Esharah Fashion Ltd

❓ What types of garments does Al Esharah Fashion Ltd produce?

We produce all types of knit and woven garments, including Panjabi, Saudi Thobe, Dubai Thobe, Bomber Jackets, Shackets, Ladies Tops, Formal Shirts, T-shirts, Hoodies, Polo Shirts, Denim Jeans, Trousers, Uniforms, Sportswear, and more.
