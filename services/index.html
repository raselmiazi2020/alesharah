Unit & Services

Al Esharah Fashion Ltd operates a complete apparel service network in Bangladesh, including a Buying House/Trading Unit, Manufacturing Unit, Embroidery Unit, Printing Unit, Hand Work Unit, Accessories Unit, E-shop Unit and Software Development Unit. Our service ecosystem supports global sourcing and apparel production for ready-made garments and custom orders.

These service units support apparel buyers with sourcing, production, decoration, accessory supply and digital commerce. Our integrated approach means each garment order is managed across planning, manufacturing, finishing and delivery. Buyers can choose the right combination of services for their product range, from bulk garment manufacturing and embroidery to printing, handwork and ecommerce sales. Al Esharah Fashion Ltd is focused on export quality, ethical compliance and responsive support for brands, wholesalers and private label apparel businesses.

Buying House/Trading Unit

The Buying House/Trading Unit manages global apparel sourcing, supplier selection, cost negotiation and order coordination. This unit helps buyers streamline product development, quality control and logistics for export-ready garments from Bangladesh.

Manufacturing Unit

The Manufacturing Unit produces woven and knit apparel in Bangladesh, including Panjabi, thobe, jackets, formal shirts and ladies three piece kameez. This unit supports export and local orders with reliable production planning, quality inspection and factory-level control.

Embroidery Unit

The Embroidery Unit specializes in premium apparel decoration with flat stitch, sequin embroidery, puff, 3D embroidery, pearl and beadwork, chenille, cording, shellie and applique designs. We combine machine embroidery with skilled finishing for fashion, corporate and casual wear.

Printing Unit

The Printing Unit offers cotton digital printing, sublimation printing and screen printing for apparel and fabric. Our printing services include HD print, silicone print, 3D print, rubber print, pigment print, photo print, discharge print and polyester sublimation for high-quality fashion garments.

Hand Work Unit

The Hand Work Unit focuses on skilled manual finishing and karchupi embroidery. Our artisans provide fancy handwork, delicate trims and premium hand-stitched details for traditional and contemporary apparel.

Accessories Unit

The Accessories Unit supplies trims, labels, hangtags, drawstrings, cords, elastics, sewing thread and packaging components. We support complete garment packages so export shipments arrive with every accessory and branding detail included.

E-shop Unit

Our E-shop Unit started in 2018 with two online brands: Texture for ladies wear and jewelry, and Omellor for men’s wear. We sell retail, wholesale and custom products online, providing a digital channel for high-quality apparel and bespoke collections.

Software Development Unit

The Software Development Unit builds ecommerce websites, order management systems and digital tools for apparel businesses. Our development team delivers web pages, customer portals and software solutions that support product listings, order tracking and supply chain coordination.
