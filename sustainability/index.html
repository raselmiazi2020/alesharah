Al Esharah Fashion Ltd Sustainability and Social Compliance

Social Compliance

Al Esharah Fashion Ltd is committed to sustainable garment manufacturing in Bangladesh. Our social compliance program protects worker safety, promotes fair labor, and supports a healthy, productive factory environment.

We invest in regular training, development, and worker empowerment. Employees receive structured coaching in quality improvement, workplace safety, and occupational hazard awareness.

Our compliance framework includes BSCI, ISO 9001 standards, and buyer social responsibility requirements. We provide a confidential compliance helpline, open door policy, and strong anti-retaliation protections.

Every factory under Al Esharah Fashion maintains safe hygiene practices, on-time salary payments, medical care, maternity support, and childcare help for eligible workers. This creates trust and loyalty while improving product quality.

Regular fire drills, evacuation planning, and fire safety training are carried out with local Fire Service & Civil Defense support. We treat compliance as a strategic strength that drives sustainable production and reliable export performance.

Sustainability Report

At Al Esharah Fashion Ltd, sustainability is a core business value. We continually improve social compliance, factory safety, and environmental performance in our garment manufacturing units.

Our teams monitor energy use, water consumption, and material waste to ensure our woven garments and knitwear are produced responsibly. This work supports better quality and more efficient export apparel delivery.

We also prioritize fair wages, women’s empowerment, and safe work conditions. These measures are aligned with international buyer requirements and global compliance standards.

By combining ethical labor practices and sustainable operations, Al Esharah Fashion creates long-term value for customers, workers, and the communities we serve.
